# Unit 6 Findings

The monthly average score query in task 3 covers January through December 2023.
The average was 15.26 in January, dropped to 12.36 in March, peaked at 25.54 in
September, and ended at 16.95 in December. It is therefore volatile, with a
modest increase from the first to last month but no sustained upward or downward
trend. For next quarter, the recommendation would be to continue reporting the
monthly average score and investigate sharp monthly swings before changing score
balance. Break the trend down by match and player to determine whether changes
reflect scoring or a different mix of events. The queries in task 1 shows each
player with their match average.
