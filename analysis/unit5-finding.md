# Unit 5 Findings

Previous findings showed which matches and which regions had the highest failure
rates. Further analysis showed that some of the matches did not have any
participation. More investigation is required to determine the cause of this. It
may be that the timing of the matches or the game mode employed was unsatisfying
to potential players. It is possible there is a correlation between the regions
where there the most failures occur and the matches with the lowest or no
participation. Results of this further analysis could provide insight for
leadership to determine where to focus future resources and investment.
